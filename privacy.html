Privacy policy

Demo placeholder. Kettle & Leaf is a fictional store built for the Funnelysis GTM course. This text exists so the course can show a real privacy link and consent flow.

What this site stores

Everything lives in your own browser (localStorage, sessionStorage and two cookies, kl_visitor_type and kl_ab_variant). Nothing is sent to a Kettle & Leaf server because there is none.

Analytics and advertising

The site loads no analytics or advertising tags by itself. If you add your own Google Tag Manager container, anything it loads is your responsibility.

Your data

All names, emails and addresses in this store are fake. Never enter real personal data or a real password. Use the Reset button in the demo panel to wipe everything.
